# notes brand kit

Same look as the Jaybulb portfolio: bulb yellow, black ink, white paper.

## The mark
A white page with a folded corner and three black lines, on the yellow tile. `icon.svg` is the source (also at `assets/img/icon.svg`, the favicon). `ios/.../icon-1024.png` is the 1024 render, no alpha.

Black line, always. No text in the icon, no outline around the tile.

## Color
Defined in `tokens.css`. Yellow `#ffca30` for accents, borders and underlines. Gold `#6b5a2e` when the accent is text, because yellow on white doesn't read. Ink `#000`, secondary `#555`, paper `#fff`, hairline `#eee`.

## Type
System font only: SF Pro, then Lato, then Helvetica. Code uses SF Mono. No webfonts.

## Shape
Small radii (2 to 6 px), flat, near-square. Links get a 2 px yellow underline.
